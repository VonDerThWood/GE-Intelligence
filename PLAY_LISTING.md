# Play Store listing (GEnius)

## Short description (max 80)

Grand Exchange prices, alerts, flips and portfolio tracking for RS3 players.

## Full description (max 4000)

GEnius is a Grand Exchange market app for RuneScape 3 players. Track prices, get alerted when an item moves, find flips, and keep an eye on your portfolio, all from your phone.

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Your data stays on your device. GEnius is open source, so anyone can see exactly what it does.

PRICE ALERTS
• Set alerts for when an item goes above or below a price, or moves by a percentage
• Alerts check in the background while the app is closed
• Optional Live monitoring for faster checks
• Quiet hours, so alerts arrive silently overnight

PRICES AND HISTORY
• Live buy and sell prices from the RuneScape Wiki, plus guide prices
• Full price history charts for every tradeable item
• Chart readings that point out spikes, crashes and the high-alch floor
• Watchlists with daily digests of what moved

FLIPS AND PROFIT
• Flip opportunities with margins after the 2% GE tax
• Estimated fill times, so you know what actually trades
• High-alch profit, worked out from current live prices
• Monster drop tables with GP per kill

PORTFOLIO
• Track what you bought, what it cost, and what it's worth now
• Profit and loss after tax, per item and overall

THE ALMANAC

The market moves in patterns: Double XP, holiday events and big game updates come around again and again. The Almanac shows how prices behaved around each one in past years, so you can see what usually rises, what usually crashes, and when, before it happens again.

• Seasonal events and Double XP, with past years side by side
• Game update launches and how the items involved reacted

NEWS
• RuneScape news in one place, with a calendar of upcoming releases and events
• Reminders for anything you want to come back to

WORKS WITH YOUR PC

GEnius also runs on Windows. Pair your phone with your PC over home Wi-Fi to keep your portfolio, alerts and watchlists in step, or move everything over with a backup file.

PRIVACY

GEnius only downloads public price and news data. It never asks for your RuneScape login and never touches the game. The camera is used only to scan the pairing code shown on your PC.
