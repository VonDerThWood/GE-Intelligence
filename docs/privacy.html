GEnius Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026 · Back to GEnius

GEnius is a free, open-source Grand Exchange market app for RuneScape 3, for Windows and Android. It has no accounts and no server of its own. Your data stays on your devices, and in your own Google Drive only if you switch on Google Drive sync.

What GEnius stores

Everything you enter (portfolio, alerts, watchlists, notes, reminders, settings) and the price history GEnius downloads are stored on your own device. GEnius does not collect analytics, does not track you, and does not send your data to the developer or anyone else.

What GEnius connects to

  • Public price and news sources: the RuneScape Wiki, WeirdGloop and the official RuneScape news, to download prices and news. These requests contain no personal data.
  • GitHub, to check whether a new version of GEnius is available.
  • A Discord webhook, only if you add one yourself in Settings, to post your own alerts there.
  • Your own PC, if you pair your phone for home Wi-Fi sync. That traffic stays on your home network and is encrypted.
  • Your own Google Drive, only if you switch on Google Drive sync (below).

Google Drive sync

Google Drive sync is optional and off until you connect it. When you connect, Google shows its own sign-in page; GEnius never sees your Google password.

  • GEnius asks only for the drive.file permission. With it, GEnius can see and change only the one file it creates itself, GEnius sync.json. It cannot see, read or change any other file in your Drive.
  • It also asks for your email address, only to show which Google account is connected.
  • The sync file holds the same data you entered in GEnius (portfolio, alerts, watchlists, notes, reminders, shorthands), so your other devices can stay in step. It lives in your Drive, under your control. GEnius and its developer have no access to it.
  • The sign-in is kept on your device, encrypted with your operating system's own protection where available. Disconnecting in Settings removes it from the device and revokes it at Google. You can also remove GEnius's access at any time in your Google Account under Security, Third-party apps.

GEnius's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to sync your own GEnius data between your own devices, and is never sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling GEnius removes its app; its data folder can be deleted from your device. To remove the sync copy, delete GEnius sync.json from your Google Drive.
